Lake & zone

A lake is a domain. A zone is a stage within it.

Lake
A logical grouping for a domain or business unit.
One lake each for retail · markets · finance.
Zone
A subdomain inside a lake, grouped by readiness.
landing→raw→curated
One lake holds many zones; a zone belongs to exactly one lake.
Assets & zone types

Assets point at real storage. Zones come in two types.

Asset
Maps to a Cloud Storage bucket or BigQuery dataset — even across projects.
Attached once; an asset can't belong to two zones.
Raw zone
Native format, no strict type-checking.
Curated zone
Cleaned, structured, query-ready.
Worth knowingUpgrade a GCS asset to Managed → Dataplex swaps external tables for BigLake, unlocking row/column security and masking.

Lakes map to domain boundaries, zones to trust tiers — central governance, domain ownership. Refine as you go.
